The YouTube hijack · 24 Feb 2008

The route leaks to the world

PTCL meant the /24 to stay inside Pakistan. With no export filter, it reached its upstream PCCW, which passed it on. A /24 is more specific than YouTube's /22, so routers everywhere prefer it.
