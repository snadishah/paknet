What you're seeing

Every glowing dot is a packet. ~60% of flows leave the country — and every one of them squeezes through a handful of landing stations on Karachi's coast before going out on a submarine cable. Domestic traffic meets at the PKIX exchanges (Islamabad, Karachi, Lahore), and local CDN caches keep popular content in-country.

Network hierarchy

Core router — 11 metros (P/PE, DWDM-fed) Aggregation — district & relay towns Access — OLT / DSLAM / 4G sites (all cities) Cable landing station (Karachi) IXP — PKIX nodes Data centre / CDN edge Teleport / VSAT — PakSat-MM1 Microwave relay

Algorithm

Link-state. Every router floods LSAs, builds the full map, then runs Dijkstra. Watch the yellow pulses: nodes settle in order of distance from the source.

Distance-vector. Routers only tell neighbours their distances; estimates relax hop by hop (orange waves). Slow to converge, and bad news travels slowly.

Path-vector between ISPs (ASes). Routes are chosen by business policy, not just distance. The hop table shows the AS each segment belongs to.
